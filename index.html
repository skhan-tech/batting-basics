— LEVEL 1 —
📦 THE BATTER'S BOX 📦

📏 WHAT IS THE BATTER'S BOX?

The batter's box is a chalk rectangle on each side of home plate. You MUST stand inside this box when you're batting. There's one box for right-handed batters and one for left-handed batters.


If any part of your foot is OUTSIDE the box when you hit the ball, you could be called OUT!

🕹️ DRAG THE BATTER INTO THE CORRECT POSITION!

Move the batter to stand properly in the box. Watch the feedback!

CLICK AND DRAG THE BATTER TO EITHER BOX

✅ BATTER'S BOX RULES

  • BOTH FEET must be completely inside the box
  • You CAN touch the lines — they're part of the box!
  • You pick the box based on which side you bat from
  • Right-handed batter → LEFT box (faces pitcher)
  • Left-handed batter → RIGHT box (faces pitcher)
  • Don't step on home plate — that's the umpire's territory!
💡
COACH'S TIP: Stand toward the BACK of the box (closer to the catcher). This gives you a split-second longer to see the pitch! That tiny bit of extra time makes a HUGE difference.
— LEVEL 2 —
⚾ BAT POSITION & THE PLATE ⚾

🎯 COVERING THE PLATE

When you stand in the batter's box and extend your arms, the barrel of your bat (the fat end) should reach across the entire plate. If you're too far from the plate, you can't hit the outside pitches. Too close? You'll get jammed!

🕹️ ADJUST THE BAT TO COVER THE PLATE!

Drag the bat so the barrel reaches across the plate properly.

DRAG THE BAT LEFT AND RIGHT TO FIND THE SWEET SPOT

📐 THE PERFECT POSITION

  • The barrel (fat end) should reach the outside edge of the plate
  • Your arms should be slightly bent, not fully stretched out
  • Extend your arms out toward the plate — if the bat covers it, you're golden!
  • Too far? Move a step closer. Too close? Scoot back. Easy fix!

🤲 THE GRIP

  • Hold the bat with your knocking knuckles lined up (the knuckles you'd use to knock on a door)
  • Grip it firm but NOT too tight — like you're holding a tube of toothpaste without squeezing any out
  • Bottom hand goes on the bottom, top hand on top (sounds obvious but lots of kids mix it up!)
  • Right-handed: Left hand on bottom, right hand on top
  • Left-handed: Right hand on bottom, left hand on top
⭐
COACH'S TIP: Before every at-bat, do the "plate check" — reach the bat out and make sure it covers the whole plate. If it does, you're in the right spot. If not, adjust your feet. This takes 2 seconds and makes you a better hitter instantly!
— LEVEL 3 —
🎯 STRIKES, BALLS & MORE 🎯

📺 THE STRIKE ZONE

The strike zone is an invisible box over home plate. It goes from the batter's knees to the middle of their chest, and is as wide as the plate. If the pitch goes through this zone and you don't swing — that's a STRIKE!

🕹️ TAP WHERE THE PITCH IS — STRIKE OR BALL?

Tap the baseballs to see if each pitch is a strike or a ball!

● ● ● ● ● ● ● ●

📖 KEY BATTING TERMS — TAP TO FLIP!

❌
STRIKE
[ TAP ME ]
A pitch in the strike zone that you DON'T swing at, OR any swing where you MISS the ball. Foul balls also count as strikes (but can't be strike 3). THREE strikes = you're OUT!
🔵
BALL
[ TAP ME ]
A pitch OUTSIDE the strike zone that you don't swing at. Good eye! FOUR balls = you get a FREE WALK to first base. This is called a "base on balls" or just a "walk."
↗️
FOUL BALL
[ TAP ME ]
A hit ball that lands OUTSIDE the foul lines (in foul territory). It counts as a strike, BUT it can't be your 3rd strike (unless you bunt it foul). If it's caught in the air — even in foul territory — you're OUT!
🤏
BUNT
[ TAP ME ]
Instead of swinging, you hold the bat out and let the ball HIT it softly. The ball dribbles a short distance. Used to move runners forward. CAREFUL: if you bunt foul with 2 strikes, it's strike 3 — you're OUT!
🚶
WALK (BB)
[ TAP ME ]
When the pitcher throws 4 BALLS (pitches outside the strike zone that you don't swing at), you walk to first base for FREE. A walk is just as good as a hit — you're on base!
😤
STRIKEOUT (K)
[ TAP ME ]
When you get 3 STRIKES and you're OUT. In a scorebook, a strikeout is written as "K." A BACKWARDS K means you struck out looking (didn't swing at strike 3). Don't worry — even the best hitters strike out!
🧠
COACH'S SECRET: "A walk is as good as a hit!" Having a good EYE means you know which pitches to swing at and which to let go. Don't chase bad pitches — make the pitcher throw strikes. Patience is a SUPERPOWER at the plate!
— LEVEL 4 —
🏆 COACH'S PRO TIPS 🏆

🦶 YOUR STANCE — THE FOUNDATION

  • Feet shoulder-width apart — balanced and comfortable
  • Knees slightly bent — like you're about to jump
  • Weight on the balls of your feet, NOT your heels
  • Feet parallel to the plate — toes pointing roughly at the plate
  • Stay relaxed — tense muscles are SLOW muscles!

👀 EYES ON THE BALL

This is the #1 RULE of hitting:

  • Watch the ball from the pitcher's HAND all the way to the bat
  • Keep your head STILL — don't pull it out when you swing
  • Try to see the ball hit your bat — literally watch it make contact
  • If you can't see the ball, you can't hit the ball. Simple!

🔄 THE SWING — STEP BY STEP

  • 1. LOAD — Shift your weight slightly to your back foot
  • 2. STRIDE — Take a small step toward the pitcher (just a few inches!)
  • 3. HIPS — Rotate your hips FIRST — this is where your power comes from!
  • 4. HANDS — Your hands follow your hips, bringing the bat through the zone
  • 5. FOLLOW THROUGH — Let the bat finish over your other shoulder

Remember: Power comes from your LEGS and HIPS, not just your arms! 💪

🚫 COMMON MISTAKES

  • ❌ Swinging at EVERYTHING — be patient, wait for YOUR pitch
  • ❌ Stepping away from the plate (stepping "in the bucket") — step TOWARD the pitcher!
  • ❌ Gripping the bat too tight — relax those hands!
  • ❌ Lifting your head up when you swing — keep it down, eyes on the ball
  • ❌ Swinging with just your arms — use those hips!
🏆
COACH'S FINAL TIP: The best hitters aren't the strongest — they're the SMARTEST. They know the strike zone, they're patient, they watch the ball, and they trust their swing. Be confident up there. You belong in that batter's box. Now go get 'em! ⚾💥
